# Plan: self-hosted runner waivers on every workflow (ruling 1006)

## Objective

Ruling 999 runs every ci.yml job on `ubuntu-latest`, and tiers_test.go
enforces it for ci.yml alone. Extend the rule to every file under
`.github/workflows/`: a job's `runs-on` is the literal `ubuntu-latest`, or ONE
self-hosted label whose `runs-on` line carries a waiver comment
`# self-hosted: <category>: <reason>`. Never an expression, never a list.

Categories:

- `hardware`: the job needs specific hardware.
- `oversize`: the job needs more than a standard hosted runner (4 vCPU,
  16 GB memory, about 14 GB free disk, 6 hours).
- `credential`: only on the Codex `review` job in code-review.yml.

An unknown or missing category, or an empty reason, fails.

## Slices

1. Test first: `test/ci/workflows/runners_test.go`.
   - `runsOnViolation(workflow, job, line)` applies the rule to one line.
   - Table cases: a bare self-hosted label, a label list, an expression, a
     waiver with no category, a credential waiver on a non-review job, an
     empty reason, an unknown category, and the valid forms.
   - `TestEveryJobRunsOnHostedOrWaived` walks every workflow's jobs; each
     job has exactly one `runs-on` line. Fails on code-review.yml first.
2. code-review.yml, runs-on lines only:
   - `scope` and `gate`: the github.com/bigdisk expression becomes
     `ubuntu-latest`.
   - `review`: `codex` keeps its `lab-string-ok` comment and gains a
     `credential` waiver.
   - `review-opencode`: `qwen` keeps its comment and gains a `hardware`
     waiver.
   - One comment line naming ruling 999's runner rule.

## Out of scope

- release.yml, automerge.yml and .github/actionlint.yaml: already pass.
- The template version line of code-review.yml and its in-file Y-8 check.
- Any change to tiers_test.go beyond keeping it green.
