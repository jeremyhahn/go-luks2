# 999: CI on GitHub-hosted standard runners

## Objective

Every ci.yml job runs on `runs-on: ubuntu-latest`. Self-hosted runners are
only for hardware-specific jobs or jobs that exceed a measured hosted limit
(disk, memory, or 6 h). No ci.yml job qualifies: the eight jobs on the
ruling-442 routing expression (lint, image, lint-gates, merge-check,
integration, soak, test-race, fips) already run on ubuntu-latest for forks,
free the runner's disk first, and the longest timeout is 120 min.

## Slices (one PR)

1. Test first, `test/ci/workflows/tiers_test.go`:
   - `TestEveryCIJobRunsOnTheHostedRunner` requires a literal
     `ubuntu-latest` on every ci.yml job; the routing-expression exception
     (`dockerPoolRunner`) and its hosted-only Free runner disk check go.
   - `bigdisk-docker` joins `retiredLabels`; `retiredRunsOnLabel` also
     catches a label quoted inside a `fromJSON` array, with a
     `TestRetiredRunsOnLabel` case for it.
   - Run it: it fails on the eight routed jobs.
2. `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: the eight runs-on lines become
   `runs-on: ubuntu-latest`; the header RUNNERS paragraph and the per-job
   comments state ruling 999. The Free runner disk step and its
   `runner.environment == 'github-hosted'` guard stay as they are.

## Out of scope

- code-review.yml (its review jobs need their host), release.yml,
  automerge.yml, .github/actionlint.yaml.
- nightly, scans and signatures workflows: already on ubuntu-latest.
